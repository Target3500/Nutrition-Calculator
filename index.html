13. Complete Nutrition Laws & Notebook LM Master Glossary

Reference: All Curriculum Formulas Clinical Definitions SARA Diagnostic Criteria
Law / ParameterExact FormulaClinical & Academic Scope
DE (Digestible Energy)DE = Intake Energy (Kcal/day) − Fecal Energy (Kcal/day)The gross caloric intake minus total energy excreted in faeces. Energy digestibility % = (DE / Intake Energy) × 100.
DN (Digestible Nutrients)DN = Nutrient Consumed (Intake) − Nutrient Excreted (Faeces)Total absolute quantity (g or kg) of nutrient absorbed by the animal.
Apparent CD %[ (Nutrient in feed − Nutrient in faeces) ÷ Nutrient in feed ] × 100Apparent digestion coefficient; treats all fecal excretion as unabsorbed feed residue.
True Digestibility (TD %)[ (Nutrient in feed − (Faecal Nutrient − MFN)) ÷ Nutrient in feed ] × 100Corrects for endogenous metabolic fecal loss (MFN: ~0.5g / 100g meal intake).
Indicator Method[ (g ind/Kg DM faeces − g ind/Kg DM food) ÷ g ind/Kg DM faeces ]Used in digestion trials without complete collection using indigestible markers (e.g. Chromic oxide Cr2O3).
Feed EfficiencyTotal Weight Gain ÷ Total Feed ConsumedRatio of live weight gain per unit of feed intake. Inverse of FCR (Feed Conversion Ratio).
TDN % (Total Digestible Nutrients)TDN % = DCP + DNFE + DCF + (DEE × 2.25)Fat (DEE) is multiplied by 2.25 because it yields 2.25× more caloric energy than carbs and protein.
CP % (Crude Protein)CP % = N % × 6.25 (Kjeldahl: Vol Acid × 0.0014 / Ws × 100)Assumes average protein contains 16% Nitrogen (100 / 16 = 6.25).
NR (Nutritive Ratio)NR = 1 : [ (TDN − DCP) ÷ DCP ]Narrow (<1:6) for lactation/growth, Medium (1:7), Wide (>1:8) for maintenance.
DM % & MoistureDM % = [ (W0 − Wd) ÷ Ws ] × 100 | Moisture = 100 − DM %Oven-dry residue at 100–105°C for feed stability and dry matter basis conversion.
Ash & OM %Ash % = [ (Wa − Wc) ÷ Ws ] × 100 | OM % = 100 − Ash %Inorganic mineral residue after muffle furnace incineration at 550°C.
CF % (Crude Fiber)CF % = [ (Dry Residue − Ash) ÷ Ws ] × 100Insoluble structural cellulose and lignin after weak acid and weak alkali boiling.
EE % (Ether Extract)EE % = [ (Wa − Wc) ÷ Ws ] × 100Crude lipids extracted using Soxhlet petroleum/diethyl ether extraction.
NFE % (Nitrogen Free Extract)100 − (Moisture + CP + CF + EE + Ash)Readily digestible soluble non-structural carbohydrates (starches, simple sugars).
PE (Protein Equivalent)PE = DTP + (DNPN / 2) = (DCP + DTP) / 2Ruminant nitrogen value combining intact digestible true protein and non-protein nitrogen.
PER (Protein Efficiency Ratio)Weight Gain (g) ÷ Protein Intake (g)Standard bioassay measuring growth response per gram of test protein consumed.
Apparent BV[ N intake − (Faecal N + Urinary N) ] ÷ [ N intake − Faecal N ] × 100Percentage of absorbed dietary nitrogen retained in the animal body.
True BV[ N intake − (FN − MFN) − (UN − EUN) ] ÷ [ N intake − (FN − MFN) ] × 100Biological value corrected for metabolic fecal nitrogen (MFN) and endogenous urinary nitrogen (EUN).
Chemical Score (CS)(Limiting AA in Feed ÷ Same AA in Whole Egg) × 100Chemical evaluation of protein quality compared to ideal reference egg protein.
GPV (Gross Protein Value)Weight gain per g test protein (A) ÷ Weight gain per g Casein (⁰A)Relative biological assay referencing casein as the gold standard (1.0).
Starch Equivalent (SE)(Fat stored per unit food ÷ Fat stored per unit starch [0.248]) × 100Kellner's energy evaluation system expressing feeding value relative to 100kg starch.
ME & NEME = DE − (UE + Gas) | Net Energy (NE) = ME − HIEnergy available after urinary and combustible methane gas, minus heat increment.
SARA (Subacute Ruminal Acidosis)Clinical PathologyA metabolic disorder occurring when ruminal pH drops below 5.6 for prolonged periods, typically caused by excess rapidly fermentable grains and insufficient effective fiber (eNDF).
